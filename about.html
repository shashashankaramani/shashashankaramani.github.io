About Me

I am a junior at MIT studying Mechanical Engineering with a concentration in Robotics. I am especially interested in designing systems that bridge mechanical engineering, robotics, and human needs. My work has included robotic manipulation, medical devices, aerospace engineering, and hands-on mechanical design, with a particular interest in assistive technology.

I enjoy working on problems that combine analytical thinking with physical design, especially when the final system has a clear impact on how people interact with technology and the world around them.
